The platform

One record for the whole order.

Trade is seven steps. We run all seven as one piece of software, and every handoff writes a signed fact to a shared record. Adding a supplier, a buyer or a country is configuration, not construction.

The seven stages

What happens at each handoff, and what gets written.

  1. Qualify

    The trust handshake.

    Parties are identified and verified before any quotation is exchanged. The record opens with who is in the room.

  2. Quote

    Specification, samples, terms.

    Materials are selected from a certified registry; each carries its assay, sourcing and carbon intensity as a signed entry.

  3. Contract

    Purchase order and deposit.

    Responsibilities, prices and dates are fixed on the record, where neither side can later misremember them.

  4. Produce

    Inspection as it happens.

    Production, inspection and quality-control events are logged by the people doing them, on the day, with the evidence attached.

  5. Ship

    Freight, leg by leg.

    Export packing, ocean freight, customs and inland delivery, with emissions measured per leg rather than estimated per year.

  6. Settle

    Balance, claims, handover.

    The order closes against the same record it opened on. Who was paid, how much, and for which verified act.

  7. Repeat

    The earned fast lane.

    A verified history lets the next order enter partway down. The record rewards the relationship that already exists.

Four facts

The questions every entry answers.

Origin. Materials. Carbon. Payment. However complex the order, the entry is always the same shape — which is what makes it readable a year later, or a century later.

Origin

Which factory, which line, which inspector, which date.

Materials

What each component is made of and where it was sourced.

Carbon

Emissions per production step and per freight leg, measured not estimated.

Payment

Who was paid, how much, and for which verified act.

Nothing to rebuild

It works with the suppliers you already have.

Most manufacturers are not digital, and will not become digital because a platform asks them to. We designed for that.

The platform works alongside the email, spreadsheets and chat threads your suppliers already use. Software agents do the data entry — reading the pro-forma invoice, the inspection photos, the bill of lading — and write the entries. People make the decisions and sign them. Each verified handoff is recorded on chain, where anyone with the right to see it can read it and no one can edit it.

That is also why we run our own trade on it first. Before any other operator is asked to trust the record, every stage has been tested with real money and real containers. The house is the test.

How we keep it honest

Rules we don't bend.

  • Worth is computed, never declared. Every number we publish is a receipt, a model or a target, and is labelled as such.
  • No tokens, no coins, no instruments. Bionpact does not issue or trade digital assets. Payments are made in ordinary currency through ordinary banks.
  • No promised returns. Nothing on this site, or in any Bionpact document, promises a financial return to anyone.
  • Every related-party arrangement is a written, arm's-length contract. Licences, suppliers and partners are documented and available for qualified review.
  • The record cannot be edited. Entries are signed and appended; mistakes are corrected by a new entry, never by changing an old one.
