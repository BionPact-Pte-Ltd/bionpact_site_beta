How a project runs

The land decides the house. Six stages, in order.

A successful modular project begins with the parcel, the intended use and a realistic delivery path — not with a product photograph. Each stage is documented before the next begins.

  1. Site brief

    Start with the land.

    The parcel, access, intended use, occupancy, preferred model, schedule and budget framework. Everything that follows is checked against this document.

  2. Model and layout

    Select the base system.

    Starting model, floor plan, openings, finishes, equipment and the shipping configuration they imply.

  3. Site and compliance

    Identify requirements early.

    Zoning, foundation inputs, utilities, structural criteria and the local review pathway — with the professionals and authorities who will sign it off.

  4. Engineering and production

    Document before fabrication.

    Confirmed selections, drawings, production scheduling, factory fabrication and quality-control records, each written to the project record as it happens.

  5. Freight and delivery

    Plan beyond the port.

    Export packing, ocean freight, customs, inland transportation and site access, planned against the parcel from stage one.

  6. Installation support

    Prepare the local handoff.

    Module placement, connection and technical handover information for the contractor and installation team.

Controlled production

Build with documented decisions.

Confirmed selections, technical information and production requirements are settled before fabrication. Inspection and quality-control records are organised for project review, not promised afterwards.

Wind criteria, foundation conditions, utilities, access and code pathways differ by location. The product must fit the parcel; that is why stage three comes before stage four.

What we are, and are not

A bridge between factory and site.

Bionpact does not own the factory. It runs the order — product selection, technical communication, production coordination, export logistics and project handoff — around a defined site and intended use, and it keeps the record.

Our manufacturing partner brings twenty-seven years of production history, eighteen or more patents and qualification documents, thirty-four or more material certifications and eighty or more honours and certificates . Those are its credentials, not ours; supporting documents are available for qualified project review.

Bionpact does not represent that every model is automatically approved for every parcel. Permitting, engineering, foundation design and installation responsibilities must be confirmed for the project location. Final responsibilities and costs are defined for each project.
